School of Film Art in CAA and CFFH proudly present **CFFH × Contemporary Poetic Moving Visuals Festival - China Academy of Art · 2026**. This collaboration features outstanding works from CAA, spanning narrative shorts, documentary films, and experimental cinema, showcasing the vision and expression of a new generation of Chinese-language filmmakers.

The event includes selected screenings as well as dialogues between German audiences, scholars, and filmmakers. It aims to promote in-depth exchange in film education and creative practice between China and Germany, and to build a cross-cultural bridge for moving-image arts.
