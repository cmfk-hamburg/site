Summer is almost over, and Ah-Fei still hasn’t completed his audio homework, “My Day.” In a last-minute attempt to fix it, he asks his mother to help him recreate a perfect day from their vacation. Determined to make the recording sound cheerful and fulfilling, Ah-Fei carefully redoes every detail. As the cassette tape unspools, a different story begins to surface—one that Ah-Fei never heard, hidden beneath the version of events he tried so hard to perfect.

### Kin-Fai Wan

Wan Jing-Hui studied filmmaking under director Chang Tso-Chi and participated in acclaimed works such as *Thanatos, Drunk* and *The Long Goodbye*. In 2020, his short films *Always* and *Seasons of Drift* were selected for the Hong Kong Fresh Wave International Short Film Festival.
