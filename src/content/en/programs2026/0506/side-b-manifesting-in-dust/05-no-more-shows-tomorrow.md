Ten years later, when I returned to the long-abandoned home of my past, dust and memories rushed toward me at once. On the walls still bearing my former name, and on the stickers pasted to the television, resurfaced the joyful childhood I had once forgotten. Through exploration, I slowly pieced together the image of the self I had lost, and at last, I watched the final episode of those childhood programs.

### Chen Rao

Rao Chen is a China-based creator working across photography, documentary film and nonfiction writing. Her work explores identity, belonging, mobility and urban space. Previous works include the documentary short *Open Fire* (2021) and participation in the series *Bookstore, Meeting You* (2022).
