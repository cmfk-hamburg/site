*Side B: Manifesting in Dust*

**Opening Film / Short Film Program**

Metropolis

WED, 6th May, 20:30

77 min

**Q&A**

*Curated by Zhichao ZHU*

Beneath the surface, these films reveal hidden facets. From maternal tapes to patriarchal undercurrents, they unearth immigrant struggles and cinematic ruins — manifesting forgotten voices and whispers.

Submerging into spaces obscured by dominant narratives, these films salvage maternal echoes and navigate patriarchal folds. As immigrant symbols are reconfigured, memory crystallizes amidst cinematic ruins—uncovering hidden struggles and whispers of existence within the shifting dust of time.
