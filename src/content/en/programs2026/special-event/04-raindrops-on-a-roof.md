Ah’e, a widow approaching her seventies, has lived with her unmarried son, Shi’an. Her daughter, Shining, returns home with a child following a divorce. There seems to be an intangible barrier within the family.

As the bitter winter descends, Ah’e nurtures a yearning for an extravagant cashmere sweater for herself, only to fall into a deceitful ploy. This event stirs undercurrents within the aging household, compelling each member to confront their long-veiled sorrows and regrets. Each person contributes their own bricks and tiles, constructing a new abode from the leveled ground, right before their eyes.

### Jiali Zhou

Jiali Zhou holds a PhD in film studies. She is a Professor at China Academy of Art, director, writer, and a published author in film theory. Her published monographs include *The Dual Dimensions of Becoming – On a Deleuzian Philosophy of Imagery* and *Firefly Light - Pier Paolo Pasolini’s Films and Poetry*.

Her film credits include *The Drifting-Away Surroundings* (director), *Beyond the Skies* (screenwriter), *Vanishing Days* (executive producer), and *Raindrops on a Roof* (director & screenwriter). Her films have been selected at Berlinale, Busan, and Beijing, winning more than ten international prizes.
