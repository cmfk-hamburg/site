Lucy, the famous early human ancestor often called the “grandmother of mankind,” becomes the focus of graduate student Ma Ke’s thesis on the origins of language. As he develops his ideas, his mother Haiyan reappears and takes him to a mysterious old house she has bought. While exploring its secrets, hidden family memories surface.

Gradually, Ma Ke’s life begins to merge with the ancient world he studies, where Lucy’s distant voice seems to echo through time.

### Xin Zhu

Zhu Xin, born in 1996 in Hangzhou, graduated from the China Academy of Art. His debut film *Vanishing Days* was selected for the New Currents Competition at the Busan International Film Festival and the Forum section of the Berlin International Film Festival. His documentary feature *A Song River* received the Youth Jury Award and Cinephile Award at the 8th Pingyao International Film Festival.

*All Quiet at Sunrise* was selected for the Berlinale Critics’ Week and the Firebird Competition at the Hong Kong International Film Festival. He was also featured as the Filmmaker in Focus at the 15th Beijing International Film Festival.
