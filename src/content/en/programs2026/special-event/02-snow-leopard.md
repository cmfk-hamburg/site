The film is based on the story of a globally endangered animal, the snow leopard, which killed nine sheep belonging to a herder. It explores different people’s views and motives regarding the snow leopard’s killing of the sheep.

Each party has their own choice and persistence on whether to release the snow leopard trapped in the sheepfold. The seemingly reasonable demands become the fuse that intensifies the conflicts among multiple parties.

### Pema Tseden

Pema Tseden was the leading filmmaker of the emerging Tibetan cinema and the first director in China to film entirely in the Tibetan language. A prolific author and director, his work provides an authentic lens into modern Tibet. His masterpieces include *The Silent Holy Stones* (2005) and *Tharlo* (2015).

His final film, *Snow Leopard* (2023), serves as a powerful testament to his enduring legacy and his mastery of cinematic storytelling.
