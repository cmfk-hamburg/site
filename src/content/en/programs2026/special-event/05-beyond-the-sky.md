A war film that walks the razor’s edge between survival and destruction. Set amidst the mournful grandeur of the battlefield, young Red Army soldier Hong Qichen receives his orders: destroy the enemy’s ammunition depot within 48 hours — a mission that is, in all likelihood, a death sentence.

Gathering his wounded comrades through treacherous mountain terrain, Hong leads them in a desperate fight against overwhelming odds. Along the way, he must also confront his companions’ anguished questioning of why they should give their lives at all — and watch as they fall, one by one.

As time runs out and the mission seems ever more impossible, some hold on through faith; others sacrifice themselves out of loyalty to duty. Rendered in the lyrical visual language of Chinese poetic cinema, the film revisits a 1935 military operation charged with revolutionary devotion — transmuting brutality into poetry, and inviting the audience to share a purposeful march toward death that is, at the same time, a march toward life.

### Zhihai Liu

LIU Zhihai is a professor at the School of Film and Animation at China Academy of Art, and also a director and curator. He is the founder of the West Lake International Documentary Festival. He has long been engaged in directing, planning, and teaching films and documentaries, and is dedicated to the creation and research of Chinese poetic films, promoting the social popularization and academic education of art films in China.

His latest film *Beyond the Skies* (2021) won “Best Feature Film,” “Best Actor,” and “Best Cinematography” in the Tiantan Award at the 11th Beijing International Film Festival. It was also selected for SIFF GALA at the 24th Shanghai International Film Festival, Market Screening at the 46th Toronto International Film Festival, and Special Invitation at the 8th Kyoto International Film and Art Festival. Representative feature films include *Beyond the Skies*, *Illegal Being*, and *Son of Sun*; representative documentaries include *Patriotic Chinese Around the World* and *JIADINGing 800*.
