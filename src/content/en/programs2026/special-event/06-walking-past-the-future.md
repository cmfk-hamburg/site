For Yang Yaoting, a second-generation migrant working on a production line, hope is faint and the job is dehumanizing. Her parents came to this megacity 25 years ago from an agricultural background, hoping to leave behind a harsh reality for a land of dreams.

But in a whirlwind that sweeps them off their feet, they realize that the ground they stand on is an illusion in the first place, and wonder if their dream is, after all, to be found with ploughs and sickles, underneath the very piece of land they left behind. They are harrowed to find, upon returning, that it has long forgotten them too.

Feeling rootless, and in all her desperation to belong, Yaoting readies herself to sacrifice her life for the future of her family, just as they once sacrificed their future for her life…

### Ruijun Li

As a home-grown director of the new generation, Li Ruijun, born in 1983 in Gansu Province, China, has so far completed six feature-length films. Among them, *Fly with the Crane* was screened in Venice’s Orizzonti section, and *River Road* screened in competition at the Tokyo International Film Festival and received the Crystal Bear at the Berlin International Film Festival.

His films have won numerous awards at international film festivals. *Walking Past the Future* is his fifth feature-length film, selected for Un Certain Regard at the Cannes Film Festival. *Return to Dust* is his sixth feature-length film, selected in competition at the Berlin International Film Festival, where his film aesthetics and unique perspective continue to shape stories unlike any others.
