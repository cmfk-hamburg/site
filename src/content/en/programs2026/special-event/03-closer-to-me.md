A group of city dwellers, bearing the weight of doubt and scepticism, leave for the countryside to practise ecological farming. But the beautiful vision they carry collides, again and again, with the reality they encounter.

On the long, obstacle-strewn road of this experiment, the distances between the people in the community begin to grow.

### Yu CHEN

Chen Yu obtained his DNSEP in Communication and Multimedia (video, documentary) at the Beaux-Arts de Saint-Étienne in France. Since 2008, he has been a full professor in the cinema program at the China Academy of Art in Hangzhou. From 2008 to 2009, he also completed a post-diploma at ESAD Saint-Étienne.
