This work is a recorder dedicated to Xianglin Sao. A diary written for the selves within me. And a mirror—reflecting the people around me and this world.

The story continues beyond its ending. The stories of people have not yet come to an end.

Now, I have become a blade that severs genitals. I want to live, to meet more fascinating people, and to break the rules of this world together.

Goodbye, Father, Mother. Tonight, I set off. Carrying your future with me, toward freedom. Remembering, hating, crying, cursing. Embracing, rejoicing, quieting. Trembling with love, running, sprouting, vanishing. Becoming a tree, becoming a seed, and then, once again, a tree.

### Nyoniko

A refugee in transit, a member of host families, a person on the neurodiversity spectrum.
