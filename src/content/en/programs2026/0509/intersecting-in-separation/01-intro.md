### Keni LAI

Independent director. Chief editor and film critic for the film media platform *The Dissidents*. A member of the film podcast *Cinema* as well as the film collective *NoHomeMovies*. Other works include the feature film *Before and Under* (2025) and the experimental short *Dimming* (2025).
