Across different times and spaces, people move through relationships in their own ways, sensing shifts between one another in brief moments, whether in physical closeness or emotional attachment. Through cinema, these relationships come into view and reflect ourselves.

The program explores intimacy and belonging, growth and identity, as well as the interplay between reality and fantasy. (Yao SU)
