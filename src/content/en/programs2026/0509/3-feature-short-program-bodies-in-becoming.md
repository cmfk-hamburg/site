Becoming unfolds through rupture and bodily experience. Identity is not fixed, but continuously reshaped through memory, sensation, and lived experience. Across these works, bodies become sites of transformation, tension, and possibility. They drift, fracture, resist, and reform, remaining always in motion and never fully resolved. (Run YUAN)
