Since the Cultural Revolution, folk organizations like Qujiaying Music Association have faced long-term destruction and marginalization. For more than 30 years, Zhongshu LIN has tried to protect this inherited musical culture through negotiation, improvisation, and persistence. He trains young musicians, raises money, builds roads and a music hall, and even brings the ensemble to Taiwan. Yet in the end, the organization he devoted his life to protecting begins to drift into official and semi-official control.

### Qingren WANG

Qingren WANG (b. 1961) began making documentaries in 2002 and has completed two films as director. His first documentary, *Game*, was selected among the annual top ten films at the Nanjing China Independent Film Festival and screened in competition at the 34th Hong Kong International Film Festival. *Prairie Requiem*, completed in 2024, is his second feature documentary.
