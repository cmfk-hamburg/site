A couple about to be married moves into their new home. To accommodate the husband’s older brother, they decide to carve out space in their lives. With April’s rainy season approaching, they wait for the night’s downpour, hoping it will ease the stifling heat suffocating their room.

### Hungji Chow

Born in Foshan, Guangdong in 2003. His screenplay *Drown into the Tide* received the Jury’s Choice Award at the 2024 Hishorts! Project Market. His works include the micro-short film *Bo!*, and the short films *Daylily Light* and *The Haunting Damp*. His films have been selected for the Golden Rooster Festival, Beijing International Film Festival, Hong Kong NEXTSHOT, and Macau International Film Festival.

He is dedicated to the creation of Cantonese-language film.
