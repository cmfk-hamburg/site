Wei, a young lesbian, embarks on a graduation trip to Bangkok, Thailand with her Taiwanese classmates. Her world is turned upside down when she witnesses an intimate interaction between her crush and a male friend, leaving her feeling confused and disheartened.

In an attempt to lift her spirits, she decides to visit a Thai massage parlor with the encouragement of her male classmates. There, she is introduced to Thailand’s soapy massage for the first time and hesitantly selects a masseuse from a “fishbowl,” a room where young women await the customers’ selection behind a glass partition.

For Wei, this marks her initial foray into the world of sexual exploration as a “client.” Due to her female biological identity, the Thai bathhouse experience doesn’t unfold as beautifully as she had envisioned. However, with her body fully exposed to another woman, she confronts her awakened desires on her path of self-discovery in a foreign culture.

### Hung-Yi WU

Born in Taiwan. She graduated from National Taiwan University College of Law and Department of Motion Picture of NTUA. Her short *On the Waitlist* was nominated for Xining FIRST International Film Festival, won the Best Director award in Seoul International Women’s Film Festival, and won the Special Mention of the Ecumenical Jury prize in Oberhausen International Short Film Festival.

Her latest work *The Fishbowl Girl* won an Honorable Mention in the Hawai‘i International Film Festival, was nominated for the Clermont-Ferrand International Short Film Festival, and the Taipei Golden Horse Awards. She continues to create both narrative and documentary works, often delving into the ineffable complexities of human relationships, as well as the emotional and moral struggles of human nature.
