After a failed suicide attempt by jumping off a building, Mads, a sufferer of depression, is expelled from her family. She wanders, searching for a place to belong and someone to love her. Her life oscillates between the turmoil of her symptoms and fleeting moments of happiness.

Will she find the “haven” this time?

### Shao-Fan WANG

A reporter and filmmaker with a long-standing focus on individuals experiencing significant social transformations. He exposed critical issues such as death from overwork, environmental pollution, and land speculation. Through nuanced portrayals of both inner emotion and external realities, he tries to foster public understanding, reduce stigma, and seek support.
