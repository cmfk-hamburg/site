In the humid town of Savannah, Yoyo, a mute who makes a living by crabbing, attends a class about “trust.” There, he meets a girl whose presence stirs a fragile belief and opens a world suspended between reality and fantasy.

### Wen JIANG

Wen JIANG is a filmmaker and artist from Hangzhou, China. Transitioning from graphic design to film, his practice began with experimental imagery and gradually shifted into narrative storytelling. His works include *Yo, Yoyo* (2025), *Narcissus* (2021), *I Do Not Know* (2020), and *Endless Fear* (2018).

Through this project, he explores fleeting moments of life and blurred boundaries between reality and fantasy, reflecting a dreamlike cinematic aesthetic.
