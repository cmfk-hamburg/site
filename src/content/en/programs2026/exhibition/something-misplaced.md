**Ye Wuji**

Single-channel video, color, sound, 37 min, 2023

Most of the footage was shot in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. The film is a non-linear presentation of what the artist saw and thought during his journey. The work explores the complexity of “Central Asia” and the confusion it brings. Weaving together random footage shot during travel—landscapes, cityscapes, and fragments of conversation—with books, images, music, and personal experiences collected over the last few years, the film links the materials to each other while interrupting the narrative.

*Something Misplaced* intends to capture a pervasive and challenging sense of misplacedness and weave it through the traces of similarities and differences between “China” and “Central Asia”.

Ye Wuji’s practice has focused on the Tianshan Mountains regions and the Eurasia connected to them. His work moves across landscape, travel, history, and cultural displacement.
