In Taipei New Park, a woman uses a device meant to find hidden cameras but accidentally brings the park’s statues to life. These statues, linked to Taiwan’s colonial past, seem to rise to avenge a tragic plane crash during a 1964 National Day military parade under Chiang Kai-shek’s martial law.

### Wei-Heng KAO

Wei-Heng KAO graduated from Taipei National University of the Arts and is currently based in France. His work moves between fiction, documentary, and experimental cinema. His films have been screened at the Golden Harvest Awards, Kassel Documentary Film and Video Festival, BISFF Beijing, and Festival Tous Courts.
