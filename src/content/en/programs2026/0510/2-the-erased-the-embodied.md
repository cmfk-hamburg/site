These experimental documentaries follow distinct protagonists — a real-life figure and a fabled character. One traces erased histories through raw, unprocessed footage; the other shapes embodied identities through constructed imagery. Together, they reveal a field where gender politics, queer identity, and cultural nationalism intertwine. (Rosine Lin HE)
