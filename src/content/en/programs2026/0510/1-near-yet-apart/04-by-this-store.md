On a summer afternoon, three women gather on the terrace of a house overlooking the Tama River in Tokyo. In the midst of this long-awaited reunion, casual words and glances give rise to an unexpected incident.

A quiet summer tale in which subtle differences in values slowly come to light.

### Kalun LEUNG

Kalun LEUNG, born in Hong Kong, studied Media Arts and Filmmaking at City University of Hong Kong, and later earned MAs in Intermedia Arts (Poznań) and Film Production (Berlin). He began in fashion photography and advertising before turning to filmmaking. His work explores social norms, memory, and language across documentary and fiction.
