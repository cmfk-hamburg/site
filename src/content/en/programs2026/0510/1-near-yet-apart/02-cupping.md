In a Wuhan hospital room in 2021, a nurse and a patient exchanged stories. Three years later, that memory was picked up once more.

### Yuye YANG

Born in 2001, filmmaker. His work focuses on the collection and recreation of individual memory and historical archives. He is dedicated to the creation of mixed forms of documentaries, found footage, film, and other media.
