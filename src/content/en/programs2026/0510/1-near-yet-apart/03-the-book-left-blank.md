Yanru is temporarily living in a countryside house owned by a stranger, while her apartment in Paris is lent to a friend, Nathan. During her solitary stay, she develops a sense of belonging and invites another friend to join her. Upon returning to Paris, she meets Nathan, who tells her she has changed—saying she is like a chameleon.

### Ziyang WANG

Born in Shandong, China, Wang Ziyang graduated from the Master’s program in Film Creation and Research at the École Nationale Supérieure d'Audiovisuel in France. Her two works are *Les Choses venus* and *The Book Left Blank*. She is currently preparing a medium-length fiction film and a feature-length documentary.
