# Call for Entries 2027

## Submissions Open for the 5th Chinese Film Festival Hamburg 2027!

The 5th Chinese Film Festival Hamburg (CFFH) is now open for submissions. The festival is expected to take place between **May 5 and 9, 2027**, in Hamburg, Germany.

CFFH is committed to providing a diverse, innovative, and artistically expressive international platform for films from and related to Sinophone communities. We warmly invite filmmakers worldwide to submit their works and share their unique perspectives with an international audience in Hamburg. Since its inception, CFFH has emphasized the concept of “Film Plus,” aiming to continually expand the boundaries of cinema—not only as a narrative medium but also as a form of cross-media and cross-grammar visual practice.

The **Expanded Cinema** section remains open. We encourage submissions that expand the possibilities of the moving image—across media, formats, and modes of presentation—including video art, machinima, essay films, and hybrid explorations that blur the boundaries between fiction and non-fiction. Where a work fits, we will present it in the exhibition space together with our curator. Last year, works submitted to this section made their way into the gallery.

Whether your work embraces traditional narrative or avant-garde experimentation, profound emotional expression or bold aesthetic ventures, CFFH welcomes your submission.

## Why Submit to CFFH?

Here are just a few reasons to submit your film:

1. **Platform for Innovation:** We highlight innovative films that challenge conventional boundaries and storytelling methods, allowing groundbreaking projects to be recognized.

2. **Expert Evaluation:** Renowned filmmakers, critics, and industry professionals will be part of our jury, offering insightful feedback and opportunities for professional growth.

3. **International Exposure:** Selected films will be showcased to a diverse audience in Hamburg, expanding visibility for filmmakers beyond their local markets.

4. **Networking Opportunities:** Filmmakers will have the opportunity to connect with industry professionals, other filmmakers, and audience members through screenings, Q&A sessions, and special events.

## Awards

- **Jury Award / Special Mention**
- **Association Award**
- **Audience Award**

- **Short Film:** Less than 40 minutes
- **Mid-length & Feature Film:** 40 minutes or longer
- **Expanded Cinema:** Less than 40 minutes

## Rules

### 1. Eligibility

Films submitted to CFFH must meet **ONE or MORE** of the following criteria:

- **A.** The film is directed by a filmmaker from regions including but not limited to Mainland China, Macau, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Singapore, or of Chinese descent.

- **B.** At least 50% of the creative team members (e.g., director, screenwriter, lead actors, cinematographer, editor) are from Sinophone communities or of Chinese descent.

- **C.** Except for non-dialogue films, at least 50% of the dialogue is in Chinese or Chinese dialects, or the main characters are from Sinophone communities, or the theme involves Chinese culture/identity.

Nationality restrictions for the director or main creative team members do not apply.

To reduce the workload for the judges, please do not resubmit films that have been previously submitted to CFFH.

### 2. Acceptable Film Types

- **Short and Mid/Feature-length Films:** All genres and subjects are welcome. TV series or corporate videos will not be accepted.

- **Expanded Cinema:** Must be under 40 minutes and fall outside traditional film categorization, such as video art, machinima, poetic images, or docu-fiction. Where a work fits, the festival will work with the exhibition curator to present it in the exhibition space, in a form agreed with the artist.

### 3. Completion Date Requirement

- **Mid-length & Feature Films and Short Films:** Completed after January 1, 2024.
- **Expanded Cinema:** Completed after January 1, 2021.

### 4. Film Duration Requirements

- **Mid-length & Feature Film:** 40 minutes or longer
- **Short Film:** Less than 40 minutes
- **Expanded Cinema:** Less than 40 minutes

### 5. Number of Submissions

Each director can submit up to three different films.

### 6. Subtitle Requirement

All films (except non-dialogue) with non-English dialogue must include embedded English subtitles. Bilingual (Chinese-English) subtitles are preferred.

### 7. Submission Links and Passwords

Submitters must ensure their preview links and passwords remain valid and accessible. Invalid links may lead to disqualification.

### 8. Entry Fee

All entry fees are non-refundable.

| Category | Early Bird | Final |
|---|---|---|
| Short Film (under 40 min) | €11.50 (Student €9) | €14.50 (Student €12) |
| Mid-length & Feature (40+ min) | €16 (Student €13) | €28 (Student €22) |
| Expanded Cinema (under 40 min) | €11.50 (Student €9) | €14.50 (Student €12) |

### 9. Promotion Authorization

Upon selection for the festival, filmmakers automatically grant the festival the right to use approximately 30 seconds of film footage, stills, posters, the director’s headshot, and related materials for promotional purposes during the festival.

### 10. Screening Fee

No screening fee is paid for works selected through the open call. By submitting, filmmakers agree that selected works may be publicly screened or exhibited during the festival.

### 11. Copyright Ownership

Only individuals or organizations holding full copyright ownership may submit.

### 12. Copyright Responsibility

Submitters are solely responsible for all copyright-related matters. The festival accepts no liability for copyright disputes. Infringement or disputes may lead to disqualification.

## About Us

The Chinese Film Festival Hamburg (CFFH) originated from the Riparian: Chinese Independent Film Showcase Hamburg project in 2022, founded by filmmaker Hanqi Sheng and artist Zhichao Zhu. By the end of 2022, the non-profit organization Chinesische Medien- und Filmkunst e.V. was established to oversee the festival’s operations and growth.

The **1st edition (January 8–15, 2023)**, titled “CHINESE FILM+: Chinese Film and Video Festival Hamburg,” with the theme “Night Walk,” screened 26 films and presented 2 roundtable discussions and a contemporary video art exhibition.

The **2nd edition (May 8–13, 2024)**, themed “Film Plus,” screened 35 films. In collaboration with the University of Hamburg and the Warburg-Haus Hamburg, it presented a special screening and panel on “Beijing Bicycle,” as well as the panel talk “How Can We Understand Each Other?”

The **3rd edition (May 7–11, 2025)**, themed “Expanding Boundaries,” selected 26 films and presented the media art exhibition “In Between And Never There” with Hamburg-based curator Bettina Freimann at the Millerntorwache.

The **4th edition (May 5–11, 2026)**, themed “Ever Becoming,” screened 35 films; presented 6 special screenings and 2 academic roundtables in collaboration with the China Academy of Art, together with partners including the Warburg-Haus Hamburg and HFBK Hamburg; and presented the moving image exhibition “Inorganic Poetics” with 7 works.

For more details, visit [chinesefilmfest.de](https://chinesefilmfest.de) or contact [info@chinesefilmfest.de](mailto:info@chinesefilmfest.de).

# 第五届汉堡华语影像展｜2027 征片规则

## 第五届汉堡华语影像展征片开启

第五届汉堡华语影像展（CFFH）现已开放征片。影展预计于 **2027 年 5 月 5 日至 9 日** 期间在德国汉堡举行。

CFFH 致力于为华语及相关社群的影像创作提供一个多元、创新与艺术表达的国际平台，诚挚邀请全球影像创作者投稿，将你们独特的视角与作品带给汉堡的国际观众。自创办以来，CFFH 始终强调“Film Plus”的理念，旨在不断拓展影像的边界：不仅关注电影作为叙事载体的艺术潜力，也鼓励跨媒介、跨语法的视觉实践。

**延展电影（Expanded Cinema）** 单元继续开放，欢迎录像、引擎电影、散文影像、虚构与非虚构交织的作品，以及一切不满足于传统放映框架的影像。合适的作品将与策展人合作，在展览空间呈现。上一届，这一单元的投稿作品已经走进了展厅。

无论是传统叙事还是前卫实验，深刻的情感表达还是大胆的美学冒险，CFFH 都欢迎你的作品加入。

## 为何选择 CFFH？

以下是选择参加 CFFH 的几个理由：

1. **创新平台：** 我们重视那些挑战传统边界与叙事方法的创新作品，为前沿项目提供展示的舞台。

2. **专家评审：** 著名电影导演、影评人及业界专家将作为评审团成员，提供宝贵的反馈与专业成长机会。

3. **国际曝光：** 入选影片将在汉堡面向多元文化背景的观众放映，让作品在更广阔的语境中被观看与讨论。

4. **交流机会：** 通过放映、问答环节和特别活动，电影创作者可以与业界专业人士、其他创作者和观众互动交流。

## 规则

### 1. 报名资格

提交作品需满足以下任一条件：

- **A.** 由来自中国大陆、澳门、台湾、香港、马来西亚、新加坡等华语地区或华裔创作者执导。

- **B.** 主创团队（如导演、编剧、主演、摄影或剪辑）中至少 50% 为华人或华裔成员。

- **C.** 除无对白影片外，影片中至少 50% 对白为华语或华语方言，或主要角色为华语社群背景，或作品内容涉及华人/华裔主题。

导演及主创团队成员无国籍限制。

为减轻评审负担，请勿重复投递往届已报名的作品。

## 关于我们

汉堡华语影像展（CFFH）起源于 2022 年由电影导演盛涵琪和艺术家朱志超发起的“河岸放映：华语独立电影展”。至 2022 年底，我们正式注册成立非营利组织汉堡华语电影与媒体艺术协会（Chinesische Medien- und Filmkunst e.V.），负责影展的运营和发展。

**第一届（2023 年 1 月 8 日至 15 日）** 以“CHINESE FILM+ 汉堡河岸影像周”为名、以“夜行 / Night Walk”为主题，展映了 26 部影片，并举办 2 场圆桌讨论和一场当代影像艺术展览。

**第二届（2024 年 5 月 8 日至 13 日）** 以“Film Plus”为主题，展映了 35 部影片。影展期间与汉堡大学、汉堡瓦尔堡研究院合作特别放映及座谈《十七岁的单车》，并举办圆桌「我们如何才能相互理解」。

**第三届（2025 年 5 月 7 日至 11 日）** 以“扩延边界”为主题，26 部华语影像作品入围展映，并与汉堡策展人 Bettina Freimann 在 Millerntorwache 策划展览《In Between And Never There》。

**第四届（2026 年 5 月 5 日至 11 日）** 以“未竟之境 / Ever Becoming”为主题，展映了 35 部影片；与中国美术学院深度合作呈现 6 场特别放映和 2 场学术圆桌（合作方包括瓦尔堡研究院与汉堡美术学院）；并举办影像艺术展览《无机诗学》，展出 7 件影像作品。
