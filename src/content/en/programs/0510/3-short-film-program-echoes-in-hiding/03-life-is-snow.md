Ota, dismissed by the bento factory manager Nakamura, confronts him. In the dispute, Ota gets injured. Each party holds a different account, so Ota turns to the police for help, bringing in lawyer Sato to help him resolve the matter. Unexpectedly, the police receive a mysterious surveillance video.

### Yaoyuan Zhang
Yaoyuan Zhang was born in Dalian in 1989. Now he is studying directing at Tokyo University of the Arts. His last short film “Half Time” was supported by the Housen Cultural Foundation Film Fund, entered the selection of Shanghai International Film Festival, Pingyao Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon International Film Festival, Pia Film Festival, the 5th NOWNESS short film awards and won the Best Picture. “Life is Snow” is his latest work.
