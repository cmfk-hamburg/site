On a foggy morning, a simple conversation between two anglers devolves into a collective panic. The movie explores the psychology of groups in ambiguous situations. The theme of irrational group judgment is ultimately shown through the perspective of the individuals in the group. Groups will distort the events they see and will appear to infect each other, making the distortion of events show consistency, with everyone in the group acting the same way.  Even when the group sees the facts, it is as if they are not there, ignoring them completely and instead integrating the distorted hallucinations with the real events. The group's distortion of events begins with the individual's first distortion of events, and is the beginning of suggestive contagion.

### Renming Liu
Renming Liu is an independent animation artist born in 2002 in Xi’an, China. He received his bachelor’s degree from Xi’an Academy of Fine Arts and is currently based in Tokyo, Japan. His works have been selected for numerous international animation and film festivals, including the 18th International Animated Film Festival Animator, MONSTRA – Lisbon Animation Festival, the 14th Beijing International Film Festival, Digital Art China, the 6th Feinaki Beijing Animation Week, and the 16th Xiamen International Film Festival Golden Dolphin Awards.
