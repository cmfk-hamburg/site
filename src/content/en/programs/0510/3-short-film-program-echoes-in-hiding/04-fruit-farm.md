"Fruit Farm" explores the evolving identity of Nana's hometown in China, from its complex past to its present. Through personal stories, the film reveals how echoes of history continue to shape the present.

### Nana Xu
Born in China, Nana's artistic journey began with a bachelor's degree in photography, during which she showcased performances and installations across China and Europe. Through these early explorations, she discovered a deep passion for film, captivated by its unique ability to capture, reconstruct and communicate the subtleties of human perceptions.
