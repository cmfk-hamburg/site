"The Echoes of Rust" is an experimental documentary film that takes place against the backdrop of elderly care homes, kindergartens, the 219 Park, and various cityscapes. Through visually striking cinematography and a unique narrative structure, the film portrays the urban ruins and inner turmoil caused by economic decline. Simultaneously, it vividly depicts the social contradictions arising from economic recession and the lingering effects of post-socialism, capturing the impact on the lives and values of people in the Northeast region of China.

### Xiuzhi Dong
Dong Xiuzhi, a film director, graduated from the film department of New York University with an undergraduate degree and has a graduate degree in directing from the American Film Institute (AFI).

"After Class" screened at Santa Barbara, Oberhausen, and the Short Shorts Film Festival & Asia, where it won the esteemed Best Short Award. "Anshan Diaries" achieved recognition as a finalist in the Student Academy Awards, captivating audiences at its premiere during the 65th San Francisco International Film Festival. "Langue" made its debut at the 2022 Palm Springs International Shortfest and the 2023 Pingyao International Film Festival, earning a Special Jury Mention for cinematography at the 2022 Nowness Short Film Awards. Dong's latest film, "A Quiet Night in Monterey Park," had its premiere at the 44th Cairo International Film Festival.
