Five blood draws, nine ovulation injections, two ovulation-triggering shots, countless pills and growth hormones, and one general anesthesia surgery, resulting in 40 eggs, NT$99,000, and a lifetime of endless concern.

### Vitty Ho
Vitty Ho is from Macao, graduated from the Department of Radio, Television and Cinema of National Cheng Chi University, graduate Institute of Creative Video and Digital Media Industry of National Taiwan University of Arts. Freelancer of visual design in theatre and video production and used to be a tutor in different secondary schools in Macao. POV Production was set up in 2021, dedicated to exploring the possibilities of visual arts and live performances.
