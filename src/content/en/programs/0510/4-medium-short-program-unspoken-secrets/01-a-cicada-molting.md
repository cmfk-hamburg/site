Dahai, a security guard, suddenly gets a strange disease, leaving him with only a head and an absent body. His miserable condition is discovered by his uncle by chance, and after meeting his mother and the doctor, he goes back to work in the neighborhood in a ridiculous manner.

### Chunsheng Liu
Liu Chunsheng, a composer, a filmmaker, from Yingkou, Liaoning Province, he graduated from the School of Electrical Engineering of Liaoning University of Technology as a bachelor and attended Beijing Film Academy as a postgraduate.
