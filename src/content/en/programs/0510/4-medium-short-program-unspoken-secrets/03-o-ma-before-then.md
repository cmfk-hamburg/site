A Chinese poem may also sound like an English letter. Secrets are briefly revealed, but at the same time the door to them is closed forever. We linger at the door, and I just want to stay with you longer.

### Mengzhu Xue
Mengzhu Xue is a Chinese director and artist. She is currently studying fine art and film in Germany. Mengzhu enjoys exploring the combination of different media to create images in a multi-dimensional way. Drawing inspiration from personal experiences and the subtle contradictions of everyday life, her art seeks to open a space beyond the limits of language.
