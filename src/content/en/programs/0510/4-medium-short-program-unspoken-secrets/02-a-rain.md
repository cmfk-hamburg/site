Before the arrival of the New Year, Xiaolin's father left without reason. From then on, Xiaolin developed a strange itching illness that made it difficult for him to sleep. The entire household was shrouded in an eerie atmosphere. Coincidentally, Abao, a senior who had been running into obstacles while trying to cover his grandfather's medical expenses, entered Xiaolin's life. Two melancholic young men, wandering through a collapsing urban village, attempted to escape their respective realities. Yet, everything is destined to pass, buildings will eventually fall, and their traumas linger in the limbo between dreams and reality. The impending rain will inevitably pour down.

### Jiacheng Li
Jiacheng Li, a director, photographer, and student, was born in Guangzhou in 2002. His parents arrived in this southern city after a 20-hour train journey. Surrounded by the rain and Northeastern dialects, they struggled to adapt to either place, and as a result, he developed a stutter. As he grew up, he moved to Nanjing for his studies. There, he found that he could only sleep well on rainy days; otherwise, he would dream of a massive, roughly textured boulder rolling toward him.
