Between 2018 and 2019, I filmed a documentary titled "Capital News" in my hometown, documenting the process of creating a documentary about local chronicles. At the end of 2022, due to the pandemic, members of the original filming crew were stranded across the globe, relying solely on phone calls to stay in touch. On August 15, 2024, the Chinese government introduced a policy to regulate exports. Individual memory, collective actions, urban landscapes, natural elements, the representation of workers' bodies—all these aspects are subject to control, echoing a process of purification, reaching 99.99%.

### Guangli Liu
Liu Guangli was born in 1990 in Lengshuijiang, China. Guangli's works often emerge from the intersection of different approaches to depicting history and events, and ultimately find their own places in installations, videos, documentaries, and paintings.
