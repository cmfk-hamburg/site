Two middle-aged men with very different looks wander between the Bridges of Shanghai. One, dressed in a suit, aims to find a wealthy woman through short videos to achieve class leapfrog. The other, unkempt and unwilling to work a regular job, hopes his fans will support him forever. Under the impact of the live streaming economy, the absurdities of the lower strata of this era are gradually unveiled......

### Dongting Lv
Currently pursuing a Master's degree in Radio and Television at East China Normal University, with “The Scream” being my debut feature-length documentary. My graduation work “Waiting for money” has been officially selected for competitions at Hishort! , China Academy Awards of Documentary Films, Shanghai Youth Film Festival, MUST Chinese Youth Film Festival & Golden Rooster Screening , and Macau International Movie Festival. It has also been permanently archived by Guangzhou Library. The academic research focuses on "Image Classification and Portrayal of Marginalized Groups in Independent Documentaries".
