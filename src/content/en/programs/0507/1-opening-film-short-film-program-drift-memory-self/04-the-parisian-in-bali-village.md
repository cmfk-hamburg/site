"The Parisian in Bali Village" is a satirical comedy in the style of a fake news programme. In order to solve the problem of a middle-aged mother, a Southwest-Chinese local news programme "Local Today" interviewed the people who have "hometown disorder." A college student who thinks she is a Parisian, a fast-food worker who thinks she is a Texas cowgirl, a bass player who thinks he is a Londoner... They have a strong sense of nostalgia for places they've never been.

### Bingxing Cen
Bingxing Cen is a video creator currently pursuing a master’s in Anthropology and Documentary in France. Passionate about pop culture, she explores contemporary themes through a lighthearted and engaging approach.
