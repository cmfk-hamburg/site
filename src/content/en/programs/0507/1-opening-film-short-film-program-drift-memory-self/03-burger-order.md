"Burger Order" is an experimental animated anthology that brings together six animators with different styles to realize a shared theme. Starting from the "burger" as an important modern symbol, each of the six authors developed their own thoughts and imaginations in their works.

### Directors
**Sijia Luo** – She develops her films through experiments and seeks liberation from visual language.  
**Shuiyuan (Water)** – She does not confine herself to the traditional forms of animation and enjoys experimenting with cross-media art.  
**Zhengqiang Pan** – His artworks feature the amalgam of traditional Chinese paper-cutting and collage.  
**Zhiheng Wang** – His works tend to explore consciousness and time.  
**Yiyang Sun** – A craftswoman gives birth to handmade films, ceramic arts, sounds and puppets.  
**Haomin Peng** – Animator whose animations predominantly feature plants in primary colors created within software.
