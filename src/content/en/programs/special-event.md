Zheng Mahler:<br>
Nostalgia Machine<br>
Curated by Bettina Freimann

Millerntorwache<br>
Millerntorplatz 20,<br>
20359 Hamburg

In Between And Never There is an artistic activation of the
Millerntorwache, curated by Bettina Freimann. The project opens
with Nostalgia Machines, a video work by Hong Kong-based artist
duo Zheng Mahler, which examines techno-orientalism, nostalgia,
and speculative futures through the lens of identity, belonging,
and geopolitical power shifts. Set within historic architecture, the
immersive video installation creates a dynamic tension between
forgotten past and speculative futures, between inside and outside.
